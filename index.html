Tolerances

We hold the tolerances on your drawing. Where it’s silent, we work to standard sheet metal practice for the material and process. Tight callout? Send the print and we’ll tell you up front what we can hold.

Quality

Parts are checked with a Mitutoyo digital height gauge on a granite surface plate. First article inspection reports and material certs are available on request.

How it works.

  1. Send your drawing

    DXF is best. A PDF, sketch or sample part works too, as long as we have dimensions, material and quantity. No minimum order. Need an NDA first? We’ll sign yours.

  2. Get your quote

    We reply within one business day, and most quotes are ready in 1–3 days. Questions come from someone who knows the machines.

  3. We make your parts

    Cut, formed and welded in Bakersfield. We deliver locally, you can pick up at the shop, and we ship anywhere else.
